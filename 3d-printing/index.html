The story

Garrett Heights Elementary/Middle School is Lauraville’s only public school, and its students in grades 3 through 8 are learning to design and build with a 3D printer as part of their computer science classes.

It started with a grant that brought the school a Bambu Lab P1S 3D printer. Today Mrs. Charles-Pierre leads the school’s computer science department, 3D printing included, and keeps students designing and printing.

Now the printer needs an upgrade. The AMS 2 Pro is an add-on that holds four spools of filament and switches between them automatically. That means prints in several colors, less class time spent swapping spools by hand, and a built-in dryer that keeps filament printing cleanly. Add a supply of filament, and students can keep printing all year.

Because The Lauraville Fair is a tax-exempt nonprofit, we can buy the equipment tax-free from any store, often for less than the school could, and donate it directly to GHEMS.
