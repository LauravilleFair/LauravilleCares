How it works

  1. 1

    Neighbors suggest projects

    Beautification, equipment for a school or neighborhood group, something the whole community can use.

  2. 2

    Our board approves them

    The Lauraville Fair’s volunteer board reviews each project to make sure it serves the community and fits our mission.

  3. 3

    We buy it and report back

    We pay costs directly rather than handing out cash, buying tax-free where we can, and post the results when each project is done.

Zeffy charges us no fees. At checkout it may suggest an optional tip to support Zeffy itself; that tip doesn’t go to us, and you can set it to $0. Prefer a check? Make it out to The Lauraville Fair Inc., write the project name in the memo line, and mail it to 2605 Ailsa Ave, Baltimore, MD 21214.
